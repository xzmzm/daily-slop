Why Did the Moth Stop the Mark II?

The Harvard Mark II used 13,000 electromechanical relays running at 50V DC. When energized, magnetic flux pulls the armature with ~1.2 N force. On September 9, 1947, a 2-inch moth entered Relay #70's contact gap. Its dry organic cuticle (thickness ~0.38 mm) halted the armature before the silver studs could touch, leaving an open circuit of >100 MΩ.
