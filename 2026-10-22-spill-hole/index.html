THE TRADEOFF

THE DAY THAT STARTED IT

A basket over Paris.

On 22 October 1797, André-Jacques Garnerin released his parachute from a balloon above Parc Monceau. He reached the ground, but air spilling unevenly around the rim made the ride rock violently.

WHY MAKE A HOLE?

Give the air an exit.

An apex vent lets air escape through the crown. It can steady a round canopy, while changing its drag. Here, a larger vent damps the swing and raises the calculated terminal speed. Real performance depends on the whole canopy.
