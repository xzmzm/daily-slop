The real thing

  • 26 Sep 2022, 23:14 UTC — impact confirmed by a 1-second loss of signal.
  • Published result: −33.0 ± 1.3 min, against a success goal of 73 s.
  • β = 3.61 assumes ρ ≈ 2400 kg/m³ — the splash pushed the moonlet ~2.6× harder than the spacecraft alone.
  • LICIACube flew past 3 minutes later; Hubble watched ejecta tails for weeks.
  • ESA's Hera arrives December 2026 to weigh Dimorphos and survey the crater.
