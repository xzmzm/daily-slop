Falling Onto the Moon

At lunar distance the probe crosses the Moon's orbit at 2.19 km/s, nearly radially, while the Moon runs at 1.02 km/s. The vector difference is what the Moon's gravity turns into impact speed. Rotate the encounter.

Ion flux rose near the Moon — a hint of a lunar ionosphere. The data became a spring-1960 report. Jodrell Bank's tape of the carrier simply stops at 21:02:24 UT.

One Kilogram of Sodium

No radar of 1959 could follow the probe. But sodium vapour sung back in the D-lines — an artificial orange comet that observatories could photograph against the stars. Twelve hours after launch, at 156,000 km out, the departing stack vented a kilogram of it.
