The sensor's field, gate widths, Earth-shine and dust flecks are made up to match the shape of the story: after launch on 28 November 1964, Mariner 4's Canopus sensor locked onto other objects, including stray light from the near Earth, before ground commands got it onto Canopus; bright flecks drifting with the spacecraft kept breaking the lock, and the midcourse burn planned for 4 December was flown on 5 December.
