WHAT THE DIFFERENTIAL DOES

The carrier speed is the average of the two axle speeds. It allows a difference; it does not decide the required split from steering. Here, the prescribed road geometry supplies it.

WHAT LOCKING CHANGES

Equal wheel speeds cannot match unequal path lengths on this imposed bend. The inner tire's surface runs too fast; the outer runs too slowly. The little bars compare road demand (outline) with axle delivery (solid).
