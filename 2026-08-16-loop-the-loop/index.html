the 2.5r rule

the g-bill same crest height · same release

A rail can only push. At the crest of a loop of radius r the car stays on while v² ≥ g·r, so a frictionless circle demands release at 2.5r above its lowest point — weightless at the top, ~6 g at the bottom, which is why 1898 passengers got whiplash and loops vanished until Stengel's 1976 clothoid teardrop put the tightest curvature where the speed was lowest. Friction adds its own line to the bill: exactly μ·Δx of horizontal travel, whatever the path's shape.
