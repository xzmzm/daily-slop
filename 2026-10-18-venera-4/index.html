WHAT IS REAL, WHAT IS A TOY

Heights come from
the gauges alone.

Venera 4 measured pressure and temperature during its parachute descent. For ideal CO₂ in hydrostatic balance the height difference between two readings is fixed, so the whole 0.75→22 atm run spans about 31 km no matter what. The only thing nobody knew was how far the last reading sat above the ground. The numbers here are rounded reconstructions of published values, not the original telemetry.
