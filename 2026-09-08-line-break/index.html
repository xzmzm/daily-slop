WHY THE BREAKS MOVE

A good line can make
a bad paragraph.

The left column commits to the next break immediately. The right tries every fitting next line and remembers the cheapest way to finish the paragraph.

Each shaded tail is unused width. The score adds its square across every line except the last. Squaring makes a large gap more expensive than several small ones.

An educational minimum-raggedness model inspired by paragraph-wide typesetting. Not the full Knuth–Plass algorithm; no hyphenation, justification, or readability measurement.
