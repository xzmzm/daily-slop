the math (Anderson 1987)

For a Fourier bed mode h ∝ e^{ikx}, the saltation flux multiplies it per step by: 

M(k) = 1 + C · i sin k · [ P̃(k) − 1 ]  −  D · (2 − 2 cos k)

where P̃(k) is the characteristic function of the hop kernel. In the continuum limit P̃ → 1/(1+ikL), giving Anderson's growth rate: 

Re σ(k) = (C L k²) / (1 + (kL)²)  −  D k²

The first term is the bed instability: a windward bump catches more saltation impacts (slope × cotα), splashes reptators that hop downwind, and deposits them just past the crest — so bumps grow and migrate. The second term is surface creep, which stabilizes the shortest wavelengths and selects the observed wavelength.
