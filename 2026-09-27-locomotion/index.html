Daily Slop · 27 September 1825 · the Stockton & Darlington Railway opens

Locomotion No. 1 — the 1,000 lb adhesion studio

On the opening day of the world's first public steam railway, a 6½-ton locomotive hauled thirty-odd wagons and hundreds of passengers — but only where the surveyors had kept the line gentle. Drag a gradient, load the train, grease the rail: one small force diagram decides whether the wheels bite, spin, or stall. The hills themselves were given to stationary rope engines, which is the quiet punchline of the whole 1825 route below.
