The plan

This is a toy model, not a food-safety check. For a real turkey, the USDA says to check that the thickest part of the breast and the innermost parts of the thigh and wing reach at least 165°F.

Toy model: a 2D cross-section with 5 mm cells, oven heat tuned to about 13 minutes per pound at 325°F, and wet skin held at 90°C while it roasts. Kill rate uses a 7-log time of 2.7 min at 150°F with z = 10°F. Juice loss is a rough 0.4% per °F above 120°F. It only models a breast slice, ignores thighs and wings, and can't tell you whether a real bird is safe. Use a food thermometer and the USDA's 165°F guidance.
