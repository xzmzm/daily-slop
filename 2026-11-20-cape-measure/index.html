Field notes

Plant one point of the dividers at Gull Point. Follow the coast until the other point can land a full span away. Pivot there and keep going. A short final step takes you back to the start.

Try 3 km and switch on the 24 km overlay. Where the long steps cut across a bay, the short steps can follow its sides. Both readings use the same tide line.

Benoît Mandelbrot was born on 20 November 1924. His 1967 paper revisited Lewis Fry Richardson’s measurements and asked how long Britain’s coast is. Wren Island is an invented, finite outline you can use to try the experiment.
