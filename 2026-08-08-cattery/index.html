Every cat's coat is read straight off its genes. The seven loci here are real mammalian coat genetics — biallelic Mendelian crosses with one twist: orange is X-linked, so a tortoiseshell cat (one orange X, one black X) needs two X chromosomes and is therefore almost always female. Breed two cats and watch the kittens, the Punnett squares, and the expected ratios all agree.
