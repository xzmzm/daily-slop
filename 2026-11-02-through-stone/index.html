THE IDEA / 02 NOVEMBER 2017

An absence
leaves a trace.

In 2017, the ScanPyramids team reported a large void above the Great Pyramid’s Grand Gallery. Detectors saw more muons from that direction than expected through solid stone.

This is a teaching model, not a scan of the pyramid: the shape, rates and positions are invented. The real discovery combined independent detectors and analyses.
