HOW THE HOUR WORKS

A warning only works on
whoever is listening at the time.

480 simulated listeners. About a third are Mercury regulars who tune in at 8:00. About four in ten are dial-twisters who leave NBC’s Edgar Bergen show when its comedy sketch gives way to a song, near 8:12. The rest drift in at random. Each stays for a random stretch. A listener comes to believe once they have heard the set number of minutes of fake bulletins without any announcement that it is a play. One announcement while they are tuned in protects them. The ending is fixed: the newscast runs to 8:40, then the station break, then a narrated second half and Welles’s goodnight. The crowd sizes are invented. Later research found the panic was much smaller than the next day’s newspapers claimed, so read the percentages as a lesson in timing, not a history of the night.
