A LITTLE CONTEXT

On 2 October 1925, John Logie Baird transmitted a face with shades of grey at his Soho workshop. This bench borrows the 30-hole disc from his surviving 1926 receiver. The sitter is our own drawing.

A BENCH, NOT A REPLICA

Disc geometry is drawn schematically. “Eye memory” holds each sampled spot until the next sweep; “Single slit” shows just the current aperture’s short trail. Real neon does not store a whole frame.
