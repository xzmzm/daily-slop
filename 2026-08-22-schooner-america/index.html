Why America Won

1. Wave Line Hollow Bow: Sharp concave entrance lines sliced through the Solent chop without pushing a massive bow wave ($R_w \propto v^4$).

2. Flat-Cut Cotton Canvas: Machine-woven cotton canvas held a smooth airfoil curve at high tension, allowing America to point $38^\circ$ close-hauled vs British baggy flax at $48^\circ+$.
