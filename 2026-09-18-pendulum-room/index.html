A PLANET, CAUGHT IN THE ACT

Nothing up his sleeve.
Just 67 metres of wire.

In 1851, Foucault hung a brass-and-lead sphere inside the Panthéon in Paris. Its slow change of direction made Earth’s rotation visible indoors. This room lets you take that experiment anywhere.

THE LATITUDE MAKES THE DIFFERENCE

turn rate = 15.041° × sin(latitude) / hour

Only the vertical component of Earth’s spin drives this precession. At either pole, a full turn takes one sidereal day. At the equator, that component is zero. South of it, the direction reverses.

Ideal small-angle model. No drag, imperfect release, or elliptical drift. The dial stays attached to the floor; compass directions do not move. Changing latitude keeps the same elapsed time for comparison.
