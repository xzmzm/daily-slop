A saddle-stitched booklet is printed on sheets, not in page order. Each sheet holds four pages — two on each side — laid out so that when you nest the sheets, fold once, and staple through the crease, the pages emerge 1, 2, 3 … in reading order. This layout is called imposition, and it is the single most counter-intuitive thing about how a book is made.
