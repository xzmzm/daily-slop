The broadcast: November 16, 1974, at the rededication of the Arecibo telescope. Frank Drake, James C. G. Walker, Linda M. French and Richard Isaacman designed the message; Carl Sagan helped. 1,679 bits went out once, at 2,380 MHz shifted ±10 Hz, 450 kW, 10 bits/s — 167.9 seconds of airtime, aimed at M13, 25,000 light-years away in Hercules. The front reaches M13's 1974 position around the year 26,974; the cluster will have moved on, but its core still catches most of the beam.
