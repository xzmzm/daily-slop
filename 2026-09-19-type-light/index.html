02 / THE DARKROOM TRICK

Less ink. More image.

Each cell samples linear-light brightness. The browser measures the ink coverage of every glyph, sorts the alphabet, then chooses a match. Dithering spreads the leftover tone between neighbors. Try two characters to see it work.

Character coverage is normalized to the densest glyph; this is a tonal interpretation, not a photometric display calibration.
