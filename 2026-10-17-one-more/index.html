A DELIBERATELY SMALL MODEL

Two successors.
Or none.

Success has probability k / 2. Loss has probability 1 − k / 2. This is a branching-process analogy: no reactor geometry, neutron timing, thermal feedback, or continuous source is simulated. An extinct trial stays extinct.

Animation is slowed for reading. Dots are sampled when a generation exceeds 40 neutrons; the graph and counters retain the full counts.
