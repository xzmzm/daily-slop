The Whisper of Microgravity

Comet 67P has a mass of roughly 10 billion metric tons. Surface gravity varies; 0.3 mm/s² = 300 μm/s² = 0.0003 m/s² is an illustrative scale, about 1/30,000 of Earth's gravity. Escape speed is position-dependent, of order 1 m/s; direction and rotation matter. Leaving the comet does not mean leaving the Solar System.

The Thruster & Harpoon Failure

Philae carried a cold-gas hold-down system and anchoring harpoons. The hold-down system was unavailable and the harpoons did not deploy at first contact. This toy does not diagnose the hardware failures; either enabled switch simply stops all motion at contact.

Restitution Is a Speed Ratio

The toy uses normal restitution e = 0.38: normal rebound speed is 38% of incident normal speed, retaining e² = 0.1444 (14.44%) of normal kinetic energy and losing 85.56%. Total energy also depends on tangential motion. This is not a measured historical coefficient. The synthetic path and forced third-contact settling do not reproduce Philae's flight.

Abydos: Darkness & Science

Philae ultimately rested at the poorly illuminated Abydos site and completed its initial science sequence before primary-battery exhaustion roughly 60 hours after landing. Its primary battery was not rechargeable. Here, a deliberately simplified rechargeable 100 Wh reserve receives 32 W in light or 1.5 W in shadow against a fixed 18 W load; it does not reproduce that lifetime or the real power system.
