Why It Oscillates

Each RC section hands the signal 60° forward; three sections give 180°, the inverting amplifier gives another 180°. The returning wave arrives in phase with itself — and if |A| ≥ 29, louder.

KILBY 1958 — FLYING WIRES

Every connection hand-soldered in gold wire above the bar. Works for one bar; hopeless for a million.

NOYCE 1961 — PLANAR METAL

Hoerni's oxide seals the junctions and insulates. Aluminum traces cross over it freely. Every chip since is this idea.
