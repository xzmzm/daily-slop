why you can't just turn it

detreflection=−1
detany rotation=+1
detreflection · rotation=−1
identity=+1

−1 can never equal +1 — so reflection composed with rotation is never the identity. No amount of turning undoes a reflection. That is the whole of handedness.

the world is mostly one-handed

  • ~10% of humans are left-handed (the day itself)
  • ~90% of snail species coil the same way (dextral); the rare sinistral left-coilers are the snail lefties
  • ~100% of life's amino acids are left-handed (L) — homochirality, still an open mystery
  • thalidomide: one enantiomer soothes morning sickness; its mirror caused birth defects. Chirality is not a footnote.
